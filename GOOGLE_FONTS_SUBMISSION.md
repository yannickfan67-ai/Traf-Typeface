**Super short description of the Font Family:**

Traf Typeface is a geometric Latin typeface with rounded construction, restrained stroke contrast, and distinctive compact letterforms derived from the original Traf lettering. It is intended primarily for desktop UI, branding, headings, compact documentation, and short-form text.
